Building a crypto investment and staking script is a complex project that requires careful consideration of various features and user flows. Let's evaluate the pages you've listed and consider whether additional pages might be necessary to ensure a comprehensive and user-friendly platform.

### **Current Pages:**

1. **Dashboard**
   - Provides an overview of the user's account, including total investments, earnings, and recent activities.

2. **Make Deposit**
   - Allows users to deposit funds into their account to invest or stake.

3. **Investment**
   - **Investment Plans**: Displays available investment plans with details such as duration, interest rates, and minimum/maximum investment amounts.
   - **My Investments**: Shows the user's active and past investments, including status, earnings, and other relevant details.

4. **Staking**
   - **Staking Plans**: Similar to Investment Plans but specific to staking options.
   - **My Stakings**: Shows the user's active and past staking activities.

5. **Transactions**
   - Provides a detailed history of all transactions, including deposits, withdrawals, investments, and staking activities.

6. **Withdrawal**
   - Allows users to withdraw their earnings or principal amounts from their account.

7. **Referral**
   - Manages referral links, statistics, and rewards for users who invite others to the platform.

8. **Settings**
   - Includes account settings, security options (e.g., two-factor authentication), notification preferences, and other user preferences.

9. **My Profile**
   - Displays and allows users to edit their personal information, such as name, email, and contact details.

### **Additional Pages and Features to Consider:**

1. **Security Center**
   - **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: A dedicated page to set up and manage 2FA.

2. **Support**
   - A page for users to submit support tickets, view FAQs, and access a knowledge base.

3. **Announcements**
   - A section or page to display important announcements, updates, and news related to the platform.

4. **Affiliate Dashboard**
   - If you have a referral program, consider a more detailed affiliate dashboard with analytics, payout history, and promotional materials.

5. **KYC/AML Compliance**
   - A page for users to complete Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) verification processes, if required.

6. **API Management**
   - If you offer API access for advanced users, a page to manage API keys and permissions.

7. **Activity Logs**
   - A detailed log of user activities for transparency and security.

8. **Notifications**
   - A dedicated page or section to view and manage notifications and alerts.

9. **Help Center**
   - A comprehensive resource with guides, tutorials, and troubleshooting tips.

10. **Legal Documents**
    - Pages for Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and other legal agreements.

11. **Admin Panel**
    - If you haven't considered it yet, an admin panel is crucial for managing users, transactions, and platform settings.
